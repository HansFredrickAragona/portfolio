# Content Rules

- Voice: professional, approachable, concise, specific, and human.
- Use first person where ownership matters.
- Distinguish personal contribution from team outcome.
- Explain the problem and decision before listing technology.
- Avoid generic AI marketing, inflated adjectives, long paragraphs, and unsupported superlatives.
- Completed case studies follow Problem, Role, Process, Solution, Results.
- Technical details stay short throughout; do not create a dense appendix.
- Outcomes without metrics may describe verified delivered capability, adoption, recognition, or demonstrated learning.
- Resume and owner files are primary evidence. Conflicts block the affected copy.
- Track every missing fact, source, image, URL, and approval in `memory/CONTENT_GAPS.md`.
- Do not publish phone number, home address, private repositories, raw private resume, signatures, IDs, references, or secret values.
